### command_queue semantic decision

Per 13-RESEARCH.md line 870: "command_queue semantic: dispatcher pops + calls
`Recipe::expand()` on each, or flattens Recipe at enqueue time — design decision for
the planner; recommend flatten-at-enqueue".

**Choice: flatten-at-enqueue.** update() arms that create a Recipe immediately call
`.expand(&deps)` and push each resulting CommandSpec onto command_queue. The dispatcher
stays simple (pops one CommandSpec at a time from the queue). This preserves the
current CommandQueue test semantics (3 tests in dispatch_tests::command_queue) — they
assert on the queue CONTENTS as a `VecDeque<CommandSpec>`, so flatten-at-enqueue keeps
the assertions valid.

**DO NOT delete command_queue entirely** per 13-PATTERNS.md anti-pattern 11 — it
doubles as a FIFO for CommandExited-drain sequencing; only the prereq-ordering role
is being removed.
