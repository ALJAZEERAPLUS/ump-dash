<objective>
Land the Wave 1 foundation that every subsequent Phase 15 plan consumes: (1)
promote `tokio-util` from a transitive to a direct dependency so
`tokio_util::sync::CancellationToken` becomes importable, (2) add the
`ProcessStarted { pid: u32 }` variant to `CommandEvent` so child PID can flow
from the runner to `effect_runner`, and (3) close the critical gap identified
by 15-RESEARCH §F1 / §Pitfall 2 by adding `.process_group(0)` to the
`tokio::process::Command` builder in `infra/command_runner.rs`.

Purpose: Without `.process_group(0)` every Phase 15 SIGTERM-to-PGID call is a
no-op against the running command (grandchildren survive). Without
`ProcessStarted { pid }`, `effect_runner` cannot construct a fully-armed
`TokioTaskHandle { child_pid, .. }`. Without `tokio-util` as a direct dep,
`CancellationToken` cannot be imported by `infra/task_handle.rs`. All three are
foundational; nothing else in Phase 15 can land before they do.

Output: 1 Cargo.toml line, 1 enum variant, 1 builder call, 1 send call. No
behaviour change to existing tests except that the new doc-string `ProcessStarted`
ordering claim is asserted by the COVER-02 / 14-08 tests still passing.
</objective>

| T-15-01-01 | Tampering | `.process_group(0)` placement in builder chain | mitigate | Place AFTER `.stderr(Stdio::piped())` and BEFORE `.kill_on_drop(true)` so neither flag overrides the group setup; verified by COVER-02 still passing (it spawns directly w/ `.process_group(0)` and proves the syscall works on this platform). |
