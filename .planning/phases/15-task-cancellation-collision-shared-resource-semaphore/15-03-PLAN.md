(TASK-06). The `Effect::SpawnTask` payload widens by one field

repo_root }`, `RemoveWorktree { repo_root, .. }`, etc.

mechanically functional. After this plan, calling `record.handle.abort()`
will (a) send SIGTERM to the entire PGID (via 15-02 abort()), (b) escalate
to SIGKILL after 200ms, AND (c) cause the forwarding loop to emit
`Action::CommandExited { task_id, status: ExitStatus::Cancelled }` even if
the OS-level wait hasn't returned yet. Concurrent yarn installs on two
worktrees sharing a repo root will serialize via the semaphore. Plan 15-05
gates these behaviors behind `is_cancellable()` and the collision predicate
in `update.rs`.
