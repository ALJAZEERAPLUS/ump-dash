<objective>
Widen `infra/task_handle.rs` to implement the SIGTERM→200ms→SIGKILL escalation
ladder (15-RESEARCH §Pattern 1, modeled on `infra/metro.rs:157-168`), to hold
the `CancellationToken` that signals the forwarding loop in `effect_runner` to
emit `ExitStatus::Cancelled`, and to widen the `From&lt;std::process::ExitStatus&gt;`
impl so SIGKILL is mapped to `ExitStatus::Killed` and other signals to
`ExitStatus::Cancelled`. The Phase 14 `TokioTaskHandle` tuple-struct shape is
replaced by a named-field struct with three fields per 15-RESEARCH §F3.

Purpose: This is the OS-level half of TASK-04. After this plan, calling
`record.handle.abort()` on a `Box&lt;dyn TaskHandle&gt;` no longer just aborts the
forwarding tokio task — it broadcasts SIGTERM to the entire process group,
gives the children 200ms to flush, then sends SIGKILL as a hard backstop, while
also signalling the forwarding loop to short-circuit and emit
`ExitStatus::Cancelled`. The domain `TaskHandle` trait surface is UNCHANGED
(`fn abort(&self)`) — all widening is behind the infra-private struct.

Output: ~80 net LOC added to `src/infra/task_handle.rs` (single file). New
inline tests assert the widened `From` mapping for Success / Failure / signal
paths. The SIGTERM-actually-kills assertion lives in the integration test in
Plan 15-06 (subprocess-required) — NOT in this plan.
